# Workflowy

> Workflowy is a minimalist infinite outliner for organizing notes, tasks, and ideas. It uses a single nested bullet list as its core metaphor — everything lives in one place, infinitely zoomable.

Workflowy is a web-based productivity app used by writers, researchers, developers, and teams who need a fast, frictionless way to capture and structure information. Unlike traditional note-taking or project management tools, Workflowy has no pages, no databases, and no folders — just a single infinitely nested list you can zoom into, search, and share.

Key capabilities: infinite nesting, mirrors (items that appear in multiple places and stay synced), kanban board view, AI writing assistant, real-time collaboration, MCP server for AI integrations (Claude, ChatGPT), tags, dates, templates, and quick capture. Available on web, iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows.

Pricing: The Basic plan includes all features, 100 new nodes per month, and 100 MB of file uploads. Pro includes unlimited nodes and uploads for $8.99/month, or $6.99/month billed annually. Students, educators, and education administrators can receive one year of Pro free.
