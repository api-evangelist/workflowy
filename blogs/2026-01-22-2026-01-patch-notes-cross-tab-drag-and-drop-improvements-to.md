Drag and drop across tabs You can now drag nodes between Workflowy tabs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Dia on PC/Mac and Safari on Mac. It works with Chrome’s split screen view too. This makes it easier to move nodes between different parts of your outline without scrolling back and forth.
